My Journal

Where it started

I started in hardware — taking apart circuits and systems at Government Polytechnic College, Chelakkara, and graduating with a Diploma in Computer Hardware Engineering, First-Class Distinction.

That hands-on habit followed me into a lateral entry into Computer Science & Engineering at Vidya Academy of Science and Technology (KTU), where the workbench got bigger: full-stack web development, databases, and — more recently — agentic AI.

These days I'm building AegisSys, my advisor-approved final-year project — an explainable multi-agent AI system for cybersecurity assessment, using local LLMs, LangGraph, and RAG. Cybersecurity is new ground for me — I'm learning the fundamentals specifically to build this project properly.
